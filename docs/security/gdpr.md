## What is GDPR?

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a comprehensive data protection law that regulates how organizations collect, use, store, and process personal data of individuals within the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA). It gives individuals greater control over their personal data and imposes strict obligations on organizations that handle this data.
